How I build a brand.

Research

Onboard the client and understand the market before building anything.

  • Support onboarding through requirement gathering and research inputs.
  • Conduct market research, competitor analysis and brand audits.
  • Build data-backed foundations before strategy begins.

Strategy

Turn research into a tailored brand and social roadmap.

  • Develop brand and social media roadmaps aligned with client goals.
  • Plan content, platform mix and audience targeting around the objective.
  • Keep the roadmap specific to the client's industry and market.

Execution

Deliver across social, SEO, performance and content, as one team.

  • Oversee delivery of social, SEO, performance marketing and content strategies.
  • Lead designers, content creators and performance marketers toward one brand voice.
  • Address challenges by optimizing strategies and processes as they come up.

Report

Track KPIs and feed the results back into the next cycle.

  • Prepare performance reports and track KPIs against client goals.
  • Turn reporting into insights that improve the brand's online presence.
  • Close the loop so every cycle starts from what actually worked.
