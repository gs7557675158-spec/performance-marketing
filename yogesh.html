How I run a campaign.

Research

Understand the market, audience and business objective.

  • Start from the goal: leads, sales or conversions.
  • Learn who the audience is and what the market looks like.
  • Keep an eye on trends that change what works.

Build

Develop campaign structure, targeting and creative direction.

  • Structure campaigns around the objective on each platform.
  • Choose audiences and formats: single image, carousel, video, shopping, search and YouTube.
  • Work with development teams when landing pages are part of the plan.

Optimize

Monitor performance and continuously improve campaigns.

  • Check performance every day.
  • Use data and trend analysis to decide what changes.
  • Report daily and project monthly against client goals and KPIs.

Scale

Increase investment where data supports sustainable growth.

  • Budget follows the data, not the other way round.
  • Grow spend on what holds up and cut what doesn't.
  • Manage larger budgets with the same daily discipline.
