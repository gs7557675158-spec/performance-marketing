Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

The Performance Marketer is a collective of three independent freelance performance marketers operating the website theperformancemarketer.in. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or use our contact form, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you fill out the contact form on our home page, we collect the information you choose to enter: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, what you need help with, and any project details you share. This information is sent to a private Google Sheet and by email to our team so we can respond to your enquiry.

We do not ask for or store any payment information on this website.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed and how people arrive here. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information in an aggregated, non-identifying way. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To understand and improve how our website is used
  • To keep a record of leads and enquiries for our own business purposes

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services

We use Google Workspace services (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, and Gmail) to receive and store contact form submissions, and Google Analytics to measure site traffic. These providers may process your data on servers outside India in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and maintain our business records, unless you ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it at any time by emailing us at akshara31singh@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for business enquiries and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, write to us at akshara31singh@gmail.com.
